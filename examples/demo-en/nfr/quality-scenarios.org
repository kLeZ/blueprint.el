* Filling-in guide — Quality scenarios                             :noexport:

Each scenario ties a quality attribute to a measurable threshold.  The structure (context → stimulus → expected response → measure) follows the arc42 / ISO 25010 model.  Use =blueprint-nfr-add-quality-scenario= (=C-c b p=) to add scenarios interactively.

** Payment API latency under load                                  :noexport:
- *Quality attribute*: Performance
- *Context*: System in production during the afternoon peak (Friday 17:00–19:00, ~500 requests/s).
- *Stimulus*: A client sends a =POST /payments= request with a standard payload (amount, method, order reference).
- *Expected response*: The service returns =HTTP 200= with the outcome of the authorization within the defined latency limits.
- *Measure*: p95 latency ≤ 300 ms; p99 ≤ 800 ms; timeouts (=HTTP 504=) for less than 0.1% of the requests.
- *Priority*: High
- *Source*: Contractual SLA with the main customer (contract 2024-Q3)

** Availability of the order service                               :noexport:
- *Quality attribute*: Availability
- *Context*: Business hours (Monday–Saturday 06:00–23:00 CET).
- *Stimulus*: Any request to =order-service=, including the restart of a single node.
- *Expected response*: The service stays reachable and answers correctly; restarts cause no error visible to the user.
- *Measure*: Monthly uptime ≥ 99.5% (at most ~3.6 h of downtime per month); RTO ≤ 2 min for a single node; RPO = 0 (no data loss).
- *Priority*: High
- *Source*: Internal operational level agreement (OLA 2024)

Each scenario follows the structure: context, stimulus, expected response, measure.

** Fulfilling an order in the morning peak
- *Quality attribute*: Performance
- *Context*: between 8 and 10, when the orders of the night arrive
- *Stimulus*: three operators fulfill orders at the same time
- *Expected response*: each fulfillment closes without the operator waiting for the courier
- *Measure*: complete fulfillment in less than 5 seconds in 95% of the cases
- *Priority*: High
- *Source*: interview I-01

** FatturaPA does not answer during fulfillment
- *Quality attribute*: Reliability
- *Context*: normal fulfillment, FatturaPA service slow or down
- *Stimulus*: the SOAP call times out
- *Expected response*: the order stays fulfilled and the invoice is retried later
- *Measure*: no order fulfilled without an invoice after 24 hours
- *Priority*: High
- *Source*: code, invoicing module [TO VALIDATE]
