#+TITLE: Code of conduct

This project follows the [[https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/][Contributor Covenant, version 2.1]]. In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions; harassment, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the private channel described in [[file:SECURITY.org][SECURITY.org]]. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or block participants who do not respect these rules.
